## 3. Full Description — English (Accurate to App Features)

School Report Card is an offline-first multi-school management and academic report card app built for school owners, principals, office operators, fee clerks, and teachers.

KEY FEATURES:
• Multi-School & Role-Based Organisation Access
  Create your school organisation, manage multiple branches or wings under one account, invite staff with one-time codes, and control per-school and per-module permissions (View / Edit / None).

• Academic Marks & A4 PDF Report Cards
  Record IA (10), U.T. (30), and Exam (60) marks, Co-Scholastic grades, and Teacher Remarks. Generate printable Unit Test, Half-Yearly, and Annual A4 PDF report cards (single or batch ZIP) with customizable school header and optional logo.

• Daily Student Attendance & School Calendar
  Configure working days, holidays, and Saturday rules. Take fast daily section attendance with automated percentage summaries and report-card attendance links.

• Timetable, Auto-Generator & Leave Substitutions
  Build period structures and class/teacher timetables without conflicts, mark teacher leaves, assign daily substitutions, and print PDF timetables.

• Student Register, Fees, Cash Book & Certificates
  Maintain student admission registers and photo ID cards, configure class-wise fee structures, issue numbered fee receipts offline, track guardian dues, maintain daily cash books, admission enquiries, and issue TC / Bonafide / Character certificates.

• Exams, Stock, Transport, Salary & New Session Promotion
  Manage exam dates, rooms, seating, and invigilation duties; books/uniform stock; transport routes; optional Owner-controlled staff salary calculation & slips; Bulk CSV/Excel student import; new-session setup & student promotion; and full JSON + CSV manual backups.

• Offline-First with Cloud Sync
  Work smoothly during school network outages—changes save immediately on the device and sync automatically to Google Cloud Firestore when internet returns.
