Privacy Policy Support

Option 1: Delete Immediately Inside the App (Recommended)

  1. Open School Report Card and sign in with the Google or Email account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete My Account (or tap Delete My Account at the bottom of the Organisation Picker screen).
  3. Review the summary of what is deleted vs. what remains in your school organisation.
  4. Re-authenticate when asked (enter your password or confirm with Google Sign-In).
  5. Your account deletion completes right away on Firebase servers and clears the local cache on your phone.

What Happens for a Normal Member (Teacher / Fee Clerk / Admin)

  • Deleted immediately: Your Firebase Auth login, private user profile, all organisation memberships, teacher scopes, pending join requests, and device cache.
  • Kept for school continuity: Student records, exam marks, attendance sheets, fee receipts, certificates, cash book entries, and audit logs created by you remain inside the school organisation, and your author label is displayed as "deleted user".

What Happens for the Only Owner of an Organisation

If you are the sole Owner of one or more school organisations, you must choose one of two options inside the app:
  1. Transfer Ownership First: Open your organisation → Members → select an Admin → Make Owner, then delete your personal account; OR
  2. Delete the Whole Organisation & Account: Tick the whole-organisation deletion option, confirm by typing your Organisation Code, and re-authenticate. This permanently deletes all schools, classes, students, marks, attendance, fee receipts, certificates, cash book, salary records, memberships, invite codes, and your login.

Option 2: Request Deletion If You Uninstalled the App

If you no longer have the app installed on your phone, send an email to [YOUR_CONTACT_EMAIL] from the exact email address registered on your account:

  • Subject: Delete Account Request — School Report Card
  • Include: Your registered email address, Organisation Code (if known), and whether you want only your staff login removed or (if you are the sole Owner) the entire organisation deleted.

After verifying account ownership, we process manual deletion requests and reply to confirm once removed.
